How to Use Coupons

Pick the flow that matches your order.

If your supplier drafts the order for you If you place the order yourself

If your supplier drafts the order for you

Select and apply a coupon on the Order details page before you pay.

01Open the coupon window

Tap Apply on the discount line under your payment amount.

02Pick a coupon

An eligible coupon is pre-selected. Tap one to check the discount, minimum spend and expiry — or choose Do not apply this coupon type.

03Tap Apply

The coupon is applied to your order. Then continue to payment.

A pre-selected coupon is not applied until you tap Apply. Applying a coupon does not complete your payment.

If you place the order yourself

Check out with Buy Now or from your cart — an eligible coupon applies automatically.

01Check your cart

The coupon shows in the Order summary. Short of the minimum spend? Tap Add items.

02Review at checkout

The discount is already deducted under Order discount. Nothing else to confirm.

03Change it if needed

Tap the coupon entry to switch or remove the coupon, then tap Confirm.

04Submit the order

Check the address, delivery option and Total, then tap Submit.

Minimum spend may exclude shipping fees.
