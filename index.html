How to Use Coupons

Pick the flow that matches your order.

If your supplier drafts the order for you If you place the order yourself

If your supplier drafts the order for you

Apply a coupon on the Order detail page before you pay.

1

Tap Apply

On the discount row, under your payment amount

2

Pick a coupon

Tap a coupon to select it — or choose Do not apply

3

Check it’s applied

Your amount due updates with the discount

If you place the order yourself

A coupon is applied automatically in your cart and at checkout.

1

Check your cart

Savings show in the bar above Check out

2

Review at checkout

Discount is already deducted under Order discount

3

Change it if needed

Tap the coupon entry to switch, then tap Confirm

4

Submit the order

Check the address, delivery option and total, then tap Submit
