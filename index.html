How to Use Coupons

Coupons can be applied on a draft order before you pay, or are applied automatically when you check out with Buy Now or from your cart. Pick the flow below that matches your order.

Flow A If you’re placing a Draft Order 3 steps · select and apply manually Flow B If you’re using Buy Now 4 steps · applied automatically
A

If You’re Placing a Draft Order

Once your order has been created, you can select and apply a coupon on the Order details page before making a payment.

Step 1

Open the coupon selection window

On the Order details page, click or tap “Apply coupon and/or credit” next to the payment amount to open the coupon selection window.

Step 2

Review and select a coupon

The window displays your coupon options for the current order, with an eligible coupon selected by default. You can keep the default selection or choose another eligible coupon.

  • Available coupons: Review the discount amount, minimum spend, expiration date, and eligible products, then select the coupon you want to use.
  • Unavailable coupons: Check the reason shown for each coupon to understand which conditions your order does not meet.
  • No coupon: If you prefer not to use a coupon, select “Do not apply this coupon type.”

After making your selection, review the discount and the updated amount due in the payment summary.

Step 3

Apply the coupon and confirm the result

Click or tap “Apply.” You will return to the Order details page, where the selected coupon is applied to your order.

Check the message showing that your coupon has been applied, then review the discount and the updated amount due before proceeding with payment.

Please note

  • A coupon selected by default is not yet applied. Click Apply to confirm your choice.
  • Applying a coupon does not complete your payment. You still need to proceed with payment.
  • Coupon eligibility and the final amount due are subject to the coupon details and the information shown for your order.
B

If You’re Using Buy Now

When you use Buy Now or check out from your shopping cart, an eligible coupon is applied automatically—there’s no need to add it manually. You can review your savings in the order summary and adjust your coupon selection before submitting your order.

Step 1

Check your savings in the shopping cart

Select the items you want to purchase in your Shopping cart, then review the coupon information in the Order summary.

  • If you haven’t met the coupon requirements: A message shows how much more you need to spend to qualify. You can click “Add items” to add eligible products.
  • If you meet the coupon requirements: The coupon is applied automatically. The order summary displays the coupon discount and your updated subtotal.

Review your selected items, then click “Check out.”

Step 2

Review your discount at checkout

On the Start order page, review your savings under “Order discount” in the Order summary.

The coupon discount has already been deducted from your order amount. No additional confirmation is needed to continue.

Step 3

View or change your coupon if needed

Click the coupon entry in the order summary to open the coupon window. Review the discount amount, minimum spend, expiration date, and eligible products.

If other eligible coupons are available, you can select a different one. If you prefer not to use a coupon, select “Do not apply this coupon type.”

After making a change, click “Confirm” to save your selection and return to checkout. If you want to keep the automatically applied coupon, you do not need to open this window.

Step 4

Review the total and submit your order

Check your shipping address, delivery option, discounts, and Total. Once everything is correct, click “Submit,” then follow the on-screen instructions to complete your payment.

Please note

  • Coupon eligibility and the final amount due are subject to the coupon details and the information shown at checkout.
  • Some minimum spend requirements exclude shipping fees.
